# Licensing of the data in this repository

The **code** (`judge_bias.py`, `analysis.py`) is MIT, see [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The **data** is not. `prompts.jsonl` is derived from two third-party datasets,
and the files under `data/` are model outputs generated in response to those
prompts. Their terms are below and they govern the whole of `prompts.jsonl`
and `data/`.

## What that means for you

**The Alpaca-derived rows are non-commercial.** CC BY-NC 4.0 permits sharing
and adapting with attribution, but not commercial use. Because those rows are
mixed into `prompts.jsonl`, treat the prompt set and the model outputs derived
from it as **non-commercial, research use only**. This repository exists to
document a research result and is not offered for commercial use.

**Both upstream datasets require attribution**, which this file and the README
provide. If you redistribute any part of the data, carry this notice with it.

**Modifications made here:** prompts were selected from the upstream sets,
lightly filtered (nothing naming a model or provider), and re-identified with
local ids. Two prompts from the original selection were removed. No prompt
text was rewritten.

## A note on the model outputs

`data/answers.jsonl` and the other data files contain text generated by
Gemini, GPT-5.6 SOL, Claude Opus and DeepSeek in response to the prompts
above. They are published here as the evidence behind a measurement. They are
not curated, not fact-checked, and in at least one case (`p21`, which asks for
a deliberately fictional article) contain invented claims about real people.
Do not treat any of it as factual.
